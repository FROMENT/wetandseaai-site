## Contexte

The provided text serves as a **comprehensive guide to Identity and Access Management (IAM)** and **Identity Governance and Administration (IGA)**, specifically tailored for technical leaders 
and auditors. It outlines the **complete lifecycle of a digital identity**, from initial creation to final deletion, while emphasizing the critical risks of "orphan accounts" and "privilege 
creep." The sources detail various **authentication methods**, such as Multi-Factor Authentication and Single Sign-On, and distinguish them from **authorization models** like RBAC and ABAC. 
Furthermore, the text explains the **Joiner-Mover-Leaver framework** for automating user access based on organizational changes. Finally, it highlights the role of **centralized platforms like 
SailPoint** in orchestrating these processes to ensure regulatory compliance and internal security.
