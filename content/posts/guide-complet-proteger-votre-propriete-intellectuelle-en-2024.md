## Contexte

These sources detail **IBM’s framework for data security, privacy, and artificial intelligence ethics** within its cloud ecosystem. The documentation emphasizes **transparency and trust**, 
outlining principles where clients maintain full ownership of their data and the insights generated by AI. Technical guides describe specific tools like **Gitleaks** for preventing secret 
exposure and **DevSecOps** workflows for maintaining a secure software supply chain. Furthermore, the texts explain the **multi-layered security architecture** of IBM Cloud Pak for Data, which 
protects information at the network, account, and collaborator levels. Compliance is a central theme, with extensive information on **global regulatory standards** such as GDPR, ISO 
certifications, and HIPAA. Collectively, these materials provide a roadmap for organizations to **deploy transformative technologies responsibly** while safeguarding sensitive digital assets.
