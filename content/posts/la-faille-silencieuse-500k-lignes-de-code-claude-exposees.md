## Contexte

These sources document a turbulent period for **Anthropic** in early 2026, highlighted by the **accidental exposure** of the 
**Claude Code** source code through a misconfigured npm file. While the leak provided a detailed **architectural blueprint** 
for AI agents, it also allowed researchers to identify a **critical vulnerability** in the tool's permission system. 
Simultaneously, documentation from **Amazon Bedrock** and official **release notes** reveal a rapid expansion of the Claude 
ecosystem, including the debut of the high-intelligence **Claude Mythos** model and the general availability of **Claude 
Cowork**. The updates further introduce advanced **autonomous capabilities**, such as **computer use** and persistent agent 
threads, designed to integrate AI deeper into professional workflows. Technical repositories also show how these tools 
interact with **standardized protocols** like the **Model Context Protocol** (MCP) to manage external data and robotic 
interfaces. Ultimately, the collection portrays an industry leader balancing **aggressive feature deployment** with 
significant **security and oversight challenges**.
