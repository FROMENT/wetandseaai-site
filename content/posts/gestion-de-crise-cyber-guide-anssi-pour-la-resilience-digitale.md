## Contexte

These sources comprise **strategic guides** and **operational frameworks** from **ANSSI** and **LARUS** focused on managing **cybersecurity incidents** and **IP infrastructure**. The 
documentation outlines a comprehensive approach to **remediation**, detailing how organizations can regain control of compromised systems and establish long-term digital resilience. A 
significant portion of the text is dedicated to **crisis management exercises**, offering a structured methodology for designing, simulating, and learning from realistic **ransomware 
scenarios**. These guides provide targeted advice for both **technical staff** and **executive decision-makers** to ensure coordinated responses to digital threats. Additionally, the materials 
address the importance of **IP address management** and professional infrastructure services within an enterprise network. Overall, the collection serves as a professional toolkit for 
maintaining **business continuity** and protecting critical information systems from evolving cyber risks.
