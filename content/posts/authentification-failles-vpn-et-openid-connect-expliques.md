## Contexte

The provided documents examine modern digital identity and the critical vulnerabilities found in the infrastructure that protects it. The first source from **CERT-FR (ANSSI)** highlights a surge
in **cyberattacks** targeting network boundary devices like **VPN gateways and firewalls** between 2023 and 2024. It details how **state-sponsored actors** and **cybercriminals** exploit these 
security flaws to gain persistent access, emphasizing the necessity of **segmentation**, **rigorous logging**, and **rapid patching**. Conversely, the second source outlines the **OpenID Connect
1.0** framework, a standardized identity layer built upon **OAuth 2.0**. It explains the technical protocols for **user authentication**, including the use of **ID Tokens** and various 
**interaction flows** to securely communicate identity information. Together, these texts underscore the ongoing tension between implementing **interoperable authentication standards** and 
defending the physical **security equipment** that facilitates those connections.
