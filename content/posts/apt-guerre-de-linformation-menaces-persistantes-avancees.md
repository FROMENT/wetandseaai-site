## Contexte

The provided sources examine the evolution of **Information Warfare** and the strategic deployment of **Advanced Persistent Threats (APTs)** in modern geopolitics. They explain how 
state-sponsored actors utilize **public information**, **social media**, and **software supply chain vulnerabilities** to destabilize adversaries and influence public opinion. A significant 
portion of the text focuses on the **SolarWinds breach** and the **Russia-Ukraine conflict** as case studies for how digital attacks enhance traditional military efforts. To counter these 
sophisticated threats, the authors advocate for **Zero Trust Architecture**, which operates on the principle that no user or device is trusted by default. Additional defenses highlighted include
**multi-factor authentication**, **network segmentation**, and rigorous **incident response playbooks** to ensure organizational resilience. Together, these materials serve as a comprehensive 
guide to understanding and mitigating high-level **cybersecurity risks** in a globalized landscape.
