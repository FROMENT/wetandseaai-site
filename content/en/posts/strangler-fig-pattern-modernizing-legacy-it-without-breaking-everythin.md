## Context

These sources examine **cloud migration strategies** and **modernization patterns** for transforming legacy enterprise systems. A central focus is the **Strangler Fig pattern**, which enables 
incremental transitions by gradually replacing old components with new services through a **mediating proxy** to minimize risk. Complementary frameworks, such as the **6R model**, categorize 
approaches ranging from simple **rehosting** to comprehensive **cloud-native rearchitecting**. Detailed case studies and technical analyses illustrate how these strategies impact **performance, 
cost efficiency, and architectural sustainability** across diverse industries. Collectively, the texts provide a **strategic roadmap** for navigating the technical, financial, and organizational
complexities inherent in modern digital transformations.
